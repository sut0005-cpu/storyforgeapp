Built by authors, for authors

StoryForge began at a kitchen table covered in marked-up manuscripts — built by frustrated authors who were tired of proprietary, subscription-based writing tools that locked their words behind monthly fees and cloud accounts.

So we forged our own: a fast, offline studio that respects your focus and your ownership. No subscriptions. No tracking. Just you and the story.

  • Your files stay on your machine, in open formats
  • Community-driven roadmap — authors decide what ships
  • Transparent source code anyone can audit
