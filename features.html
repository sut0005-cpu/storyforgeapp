Features

A quiet studio with serious craft

Four pillars that take you from blank page to published book — without subscriptions or clutter.

01 — Draft

Distraction-free writing

Clean drafting modes with smart chapter outlining and effortless scene management. Focus mode fades everything but the sentence you're writing. Typewriter scrolling keeps your place. Word-count goals, session timers, and a full-screen calm view keep momentum without nagging.

  • Chapter + scene binder with drag-and-drop reordering
  • Focus, typewriter, and full-screen drafting modes
  • Corkboard outline, notes, and per-scene targets
  • Autosave locally — never lose a paragraph again
02 — Design

Built-in font creation

Design bespoke typography directly inside your project for a unique aesthetic. Shape chapter headings, drop caps, and body text with live previews — then embed your typeface straight into your exports so your book looks like yours everywhere.

  • Visual type designer with live book-page preview
  • Drop caps, running heads, and title-page styles
  • Import and pair open-license fonts freely
  • Fonts travel with your project file — no missing type
03 — Publish

Versatile export options

Publish to EPUB, PDF, and print-ready manuscript formats in one click. Presets for Kindle, Apple Books, trade paperback, and standard manuscript format mean you don't need three extra tools — or a designer on retainer — to ship.

  • One-click EPUB, styled PDF, and DOCX manuscript export
  • Print-ready presets with trim, bleed, and margins
  • Automatic table of contents, page numbers, front matter
  • Validate-before-you-publish checks for retailers
04 — Own

100% free and open-source

No hidden subscriptions, no telemetry, complete offline ownership. StoryForge runs entirely on your machine, stores your work in open formats, and its source code is public for anyone to inspect, improve, or fork.

  • Free forever — no accounts, no paywalled features
  • Works fully offline; zero tracking or analytics
  • Open file formats you can open anywhere
  • Community-owned roadmap under an open license
