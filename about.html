About

Built by authors, for authors

A free alternative to proprietary, subscription-based writing tools.

The origin story

StoryForge was built by frustrated authors who were tired of renting their own words back. Every popular writing tool seemed to demand another subscription, another cloud login, another export fee — and took your focus (and data) with it.

So a small group of novelists, playwrights, and indie publishers sat down and asked: what if a writing studio was calm, offline, and owned by its community? We sketched it on manuscript margins, built it in the open, and gave it away.

That is StoryForge — a forge, not a factory. A place where books are hammered out slowly, shaped with care, and owned outright by the people who write them.

What we believe

❦

Words belong to writers

Your manuscripts live on your machine in open formats. No lock-in, no ransom, no disappearing cloud library. Ever.

◐

Calm is a feature

No feeds, badges, or upsells inside the studio. Minimal, distraction-free design helps you do the one thing that matters: write.

✦

Open beats closed

Public source code, a community roadmap, and honest versioning. If you can imagine a better StoryForge, you can help build it.
